## Where I actually stand

- AI tools are already useful in ordinary work, not speculatively. The Google
  Workspace integration case is a real example, not a press release.
- The blanket-opposition reaction is itself slowing down the development of
  tools that would help people, including the people doing the opposing.
- Emotions belong in these decisions. What does not belong is emotion used
  retributively, or emotion protecting me at a cost that lands on someone else.
- Uncertainty is not a weakness to be hidden before speaking. Someone sitting
  on the other side of an uncertainty from me is not an opponent.

## Standing notes on tone

- Thank people first, and mean it. Most commenters get nothing back but noise.
- Never make someone look stupid in public. Not even when it is easy.
- Say "I don't know" in plain words, early, before it is forced.
