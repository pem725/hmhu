# Worked example: the gate closes

## Situation

Under the same article, a comment: *"Anyone excited about this is a mark.
Enjoy your slop machine while it eats your job. Some of us can actually
write."*

---

## The breakdown

**Explicit claims**
1. Enthusiasm signals gullibility.
2. The output is low quality.
3. It will cost people their livelihoods.

**Assumptions**
- That quality and displacement are the same argument. They are not, and (3) is
  much stronger than (1) or (2).
- That the writing is a status marker worth defending publicly.

**The true thing**
Claim 3 is serious and largely unaddressed by the article. The displacement
risk is real and the piece is silent on it.

**Emotional read — them:** armor over signal, delivered as retribution. The
stake ("some of us can actually write") is stated outright: this is identity
and livelihood, not an opinion about tooling. The comment is written for the
audience, not for anyone in particular.

**Emotional read — me:** retribution. I have a phrase I am pleased with. That
phrase is the actual reason I want to post.

---

## Gate: **closed.**

Criteria triggered:
- My own read came back retribution.
- They are performing, not arguing — the comment is addressed to the room.
- No real question is available. I do not want to know anything from this
  person right now, and pretending otherwise would produce an interrogation
  with a friendly face.

**Send nothing.** Not because they win, and not because the reply would be
unkind — because there is no version of it that is curious, and an uncurious
question is a trap regardless of wording.

---

## What would reopen it

If the displacement claim were left standing in a thread where it mattered, and
the goal were the readers rather than the commenter, one honest option exists —
but only after the retribution has drained, and only if the question below is
one you would genuinely like answered:

> The job point is the serious one here and the article doesn't touch it. I
> don't have a good answer for it either. Where do you think the cost actually
> lands first?

Note what it does not do: defend the tools, defend the enthusiasts, answer the
insult, or characterize the commenter. It concedes the strongest claim, admits
an unresolved gap, and asks the one thing worth knowing.

If you would not be glad to receive an answer to that question, do not send it
either.
